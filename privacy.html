NoteStash Privacy Policy

English

Last updated: October 16, 2026

NoteStash is a Chrome extension that saves your notes, images and files to a folder in your own Google Drive. It has no server of its own. The developer never receives, sees or stores your data.

What NoteStash accesses

  • Your Google account's email address and display name. They are read once when you sign in and shown in the extension so you know which account you are using. They are stored only in your browser.
  • Files that NoteStash creates in your Google Drive. NoteStash uses the drive.file permission, so Google only lets it see and change files that NoteStash itself created. It cannot see any of your other Drive files.
  • The notes, images and files you add. They go directly from your browser to your Google Drive.

What is stored on your computer

  • The Google access token is kept in Chrome's session storage and is discarded when the browser closes.
  • Your settings, the account name shown in the extension, and edits waiting to be uploaded are kept in Chrome's extension storage.
  • Copies of images are cached locally so they load quickly.

Signing out from the extension, or removing it, clears this local data.

What NoteStash does not do

  • It does not send your data to the developer or to any server other than Google's own APIs.
  • It has no analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.
  • It does not sell or share your data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising, credit or lending decisions.
  • It does not let any person read your data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

NoteStash's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Your notes are ordinary files in your Google Drive. Delete the NoteStash folder in Drive to delete them. To revoke NoteStash's access, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Donations

The "Buy me a coffee" button opens PayPal in a new tab. NoteStash does not see or handle any payment information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

简体中文

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 16 日

NoteStash 是一个 Chrome 扩展，把你的笔记、图片和文件保存到你自己 Google Drive 里的一个文件夹。它没有自己的服务器，开发者收不到、也看不到你的任何数据。

NoteStash 访问的内容

  • 你的 Google 账号邮箱和显示名。登录时读取一次，只在扩展中显示，方便你确认当前使用的账号。这些信息仅保存在你的浏览器中。
  • NoteStash 在你 Google Drive 中创建的文件。NoteStash 使用 drive.file 权限，Google 只允许它查看和修改它自己创建的文件，无法查看你 Drive 中的其他文件。
  • 你添加的笔记、图片和文件。这些内容直接从浏览器传到你的 Google Drive。

保存在你电脑上的内容

  • Google 访问令牌保存在 Chrome 的会话存储中，浏览器关闭后会被清除。
  • 设置、扩展中显示的账号名和等待上传的修改保存在 Chrome 的扩展存储中。
  • 图片副本会在本地缓存，以便更快加载。

从扩展退出登录或卸载扩展，会清除这些本地数据。

NoteStash 不做的事

  • 不把你的数据发给开发者，也不发送到 Google API 之外的服务器。
  • 没有统计、追踪、广告或远程代码。
  • 不向任何人出售或共享你的数据，也不用于广告、信用评估或贷款决策。
  • 不允许任何人通过 NoteStash 读取你的数据。

Google API 服务用户数据政策

NoteStash 对 Google API 所获信息的使用和传输遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其中的 Limited Use（受限使用）要求。

删除你的数据

笔记是你 Google Drive 中的普通文件。在 Drive 中删除 NoteStash 文件夹即可删除这些数据。撤销 NoteStash 的访问权限，请前往 myaccount.google.com/permissions。

自愿支持

“Buy me a coffee”按钮会在新标签页打开 PayPal。NoteStash 不会看到或处理任何支付信息。

政策变更

如果政策发生变更，新版本会发布在此页面，并更新日期。
