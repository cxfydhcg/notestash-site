What it does

  • Click the toolbar icon to jot a note, paste a screenshot or drop in a file.
  • Organize notes in tabs. Open the full-tab view for longer writing or big uploads.
  • Everything is saved to a NoteStash folder in your Google Drive, so the same notes show up on every computer where you sign in.
  • Preview PDFs and images without leaving the extension.
  • Upload images and files up to 100 MB each, with upload progress. Keep NoteStash open until the upload finishes.

Private by design

There is no NoteStash server, no analytics and no ads. NoteStash can only see the files it created in your Drive. Read the privacy policy.

Free to use

NoteStash is free. The optional "Buy me a coffee" button lets you support its development.
